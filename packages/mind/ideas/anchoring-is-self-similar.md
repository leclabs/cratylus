# Anchoring Is Self-Similar

[[precise-circumscription]] (fitting a _name_ to an exemplar) and [[densest-faithful-point]] (fitting an _expression_ to its content) are **one operation applied at every grain** — token, phrase, fragment, cell, document, corpus. The method is scale-invariant: the same two criteria decide a slug, a sentence, and the shape of the whole corpus.

Because it is scale-invariant, the operation reaches the library's own artifacts — which is what makes [[self-application-is-mandatory]] possible at all.
