# Anchor (gloss)

An **anchor** is the dense name that labels an exemplar — the single word or short phrase a fragment is filed under. It works because a name, read by a capable mind, fires a whole structured understanding (its _latent priors_): say "bulkhead" to an engineer and the isolation principle arrives whole, unstated.

The _right_ anchor is the **smallest exact name** whose evoked understanding matches the idea precisely. Choosing it is the act of [[precise-circumscription]]. The corpus is organised by anchors: each cell's filename slug _is_ its anchor.
