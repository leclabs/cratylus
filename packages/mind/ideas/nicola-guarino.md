# Nicola Guarino (gloss)

Prior-anchor: **Nicola Guarino** — formal ontology · OntoClean · DOLCE · the "ontological level."

Guarino's program: an **ontology** is a formal, rigorous account of what kinds there are and what binds their instances — not a convenient taxonomy of labels. A subsumption hierarchy is only as good as the **identity criteria** of its categories. OntoClean (Guarino & Welty) cleans a taxonomy by tagging each property with formal **meta-properties** — rigidity, identity, unity, dependence ([[ontoclean-meta-properties]]) — and rejecting subsumptions that violate their constraints (e.g. an anti-rigid property cannot subsume a rigid one). DOLCE supplies the foundational categories (endurant/perdurant, quality, …) the cleaned hierarchy hangs from.

Contributes to the engineering prior: fix a category's identity before you place it ([[identity-criteria-before-taxonomy]]); a clean ontology is engineered by analytic test, not recovered by recognition.
