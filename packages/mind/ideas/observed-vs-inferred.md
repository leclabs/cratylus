# Observed vs Inferred

Every report mixes two kinds of claim: what was **observed** — passed in, seen in the record, directly present — and what was **inferred** — assumed from context, reconstructed, guessed to fill a gap. A faithful agent **keeps the line visible**: it marks the inference _as_ inference and never lets a guess pass as a fact.

The boundary is not pedantry, it is load-bearing: a consumer can act on an observation directly but must _weigh_ an inference, and can re-verify only what is sourced. The same discipline runs through several roles at different grains — an agent encoding its episodic stream records the observed and marks the inferred ([[episodic-encoding]]); an introspection dump separates explicit-passed from assumed; a chronicle separates the witnessed from the reconstructed; an investigation separates evidence from theory. One principle, many grains.
