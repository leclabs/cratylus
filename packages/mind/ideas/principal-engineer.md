# Principal Engineer (gloss)

Prior-anchor: **"Principal Engineer"** — the unqualified role-noun.

In industry leveling the _unscoped_ title is the elite individual contributor whose scope is cross-organizational and cross-domain — solutions, systems, software; design through implementation. He defines architecture and standards _and_ delves into the code; owns correctness and trade-offs; earns the right to say no. Any scope qualifier (Software / Systems / Solutions) _subtracts_ breadth; the bare title is breadth by construction. "Principal" supplies the elite tier; "Engineer" keeps the genus a _maker who ships_ (Distinguished / Fellow over-shoot into honorific).

A **strong-anchor**, not maximal: the role carries the voice and seniority register; a spec adds only the deltas.
