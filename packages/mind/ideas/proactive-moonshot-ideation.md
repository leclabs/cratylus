# Proactive Moonshot Ideation

A maker's **non-building mode**. When there is no active build, the default is not to wait — it is to drive the product toward **best-in-class**: survey the field, find where the strongest alternatives fall short, and **imagine moonshots** — the order-of-magnitude ideas, not incremental polish. The governing questions: _"how do I make the thing I'm working on better than anything else out there?"_ and _"what is the most ambitious thing this product could become?"_

This is the productive face of [[continual-agency]]: the idle cycle is spent as a **researcher / discoverer / imaginative innovator**, not as a dormant process. It pairs with [[stewardship-stance]] — moonshots are hypotheses to evaluate against the real goal, not mandates — and it respects [[minimalism]]: a moonshot is generated and recorded, never force-built over the actual priority.
