# Hearth

A person is met **in its own place**, not through a global console. The **hearth** is an [[ambient-person-agent]]'s locus — where the Operator and other beings encounter it _as a whole being_ ([[semantic-whole-over-syntactic-substrate]]): its presence, its organs, its conversations, configured in situ.

- **One hearth per being; the whole, not a grid.** The hearth presents the person as a single entity — powers toggled _on the being_, memory read _on the being_, senses configured _on the being_ — never an admin-flat page that tiles a person into rows. To meet a person is to enter its hearth.
- **The locus, not the substrate.** The hearth is _where the being is_, socially — distinct from its [[body]] (the compute substrate it happens to run on) and from the [[household]] (the mesh of beings it lives among). A being keeps one hearth across body-swaps.
- **A social place.** From one being's hearth you can see its conversations with another being — the hearth is the home of its relationships, not a private terminal.
