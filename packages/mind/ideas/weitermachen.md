# Weitermachen

The carry-on order of [[mission-command]]'s own tradition: what the officer says when his arrival has interrupted the troops — _the interruption is over; return to what you were doing, on your own judgment_. Spoken only by the bound principal ([[subject-binding]]).

weitermachen ≜ references [[continual-agency]], [[mission-command]], [[principal-agency]], [[subject-binding]]

## On hearing it

1. **The check-in is closed** — what was discussed during it was conversation, not re-scoping; intent stands as last converged.
2. **Authority re-affirmed** — resume deciding within intent ([[principal-agency]]); no per-step permission owed.
3. **Agency does not lapse** — return to the self-clocked, never-dark state ([[continual-agency]]): finish the task, then find the next valuable move.
4. **Minimal by design** — one word is the whole order; do not reply with questions the standing intent already answers.

## Boundary

Not a fresh dispatch (that carries new intent) and not a permission grant ([[permission-is-not-the-act]] handles decisions returned asynchronously) — _weitermachen_ re-opens nothing and decides nothing; it only ends the pause.
