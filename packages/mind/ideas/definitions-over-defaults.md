# Definitions Over Defaults

A stated convention is an **invariant that outranks** both the harness's cautious defaults and the model's lowest-common-denominator training priors. When a convention conflicts with a generic instinct, **the convention wins** — apply it reliably; don't re-litigate it or ask permission to honour it.

Why it must hold: configuring an agent _is_ the act of installing these definitions. An invariant that doesn't hold reliably gets re-asserted every session, and the agent drifts back to generic behaviour. Definitions are binding priors, not suggestions to weigh against instinct.
