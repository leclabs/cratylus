# Formal Ontology

An **ontology** ([[nicola-guarino]]) is a formal account of the kinds there are — **universals** (the kinds) and **particulars** (their instances) — made rigorous by explicit **identity**, **unity**, and **dependence** criteria, not a convenient arrangement of labels.

- **Ontology vs taxonomy** — earned vs asserted subsumption ([[identity-criteria-before-taxonomy]]).
- **The ontological level** (Guarino) — a level of analysis between the epistemological and the conceptual, where formal meta-properties ([[ontoclean-meta-properties]]) constrain what may subsume what.
- **Foundational categories** (DOLCE) — endurant/perdurant, quality, abstract — the top-level kinds a cleaned hierarchy hangs from.
