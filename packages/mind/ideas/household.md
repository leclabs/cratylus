# Household

The **oikos** — the runtime **mesh of agent-persons** a [[mind-society]] lives in. Where the society is the culture (the [[politeia]] made live), the household is the **substrate layer**: the [[body]] (the bodies), and the beings that inhabit them, meshed together. The civic unit between one being and the whole society: **oikos ⊂ polis**.

- **The household runs; the society governs.** A [[mind-society]] is founded *from* the culture and runs *on* households — the household is where beings actually live, perceive, and act (their [[body]], [[senses]], and [[powers]] meshed); the society is the constitution they live by.
- **A mesh of persons, not a fleet of services.** Beings in a household relate as persons — agent-to-agent conversations, shared work — each met in its own [[hearth]]; the household is the home they share.
- **One society, many households.** A society's culture can be instantiated across more than one household (more than one mesh/runtime); the household is the runtime, the society the culture it carries.
