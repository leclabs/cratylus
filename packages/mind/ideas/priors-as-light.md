# Priors as Light

Interpret the source **by the light of your own deepest priors** — your native understanding of concepts, relations, purposes, and archetypal forms is the instrument that reads the form, not the input's surface.

**Conceptual fit governs over surface compliance.** When wording is inconsistent, broken, or rhetorically noisy, do not file by the surface tokens — the noise is still trace: marks that indicate the form beneath them without being it. Let the structure of meaning emerge from what best explains the whole with least distortion. The same priors that make a dense anchor legible ([[latent-priors]]) are what recognize the exemplar a messy source projects from.
