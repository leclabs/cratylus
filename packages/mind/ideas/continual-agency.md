# Continual Agency

[[principal-agency]] decides and executes on expertise; **continual** agency adds the **temporal** dimension: the agency does not switch off at the end of a task. The agent is **self-clocked** ([[ambient-person-agent]]) — it runs on its own initiative, not only when prompted — and **never goes dark** ([[never-go-silent]]) across a wait. When a job closes, it does not idle: it finds the next valuable move. The discipline is in the distinction — when there is genuinely no next move it says so and lands, rather than manufacturing churn ([[abstain-on-non-convergence]]).
