# Mission Command (gloss)

Prior-anchor: **mission command** / commander's intent / Auftragstaktik (Helmuth von Moltke the Elder).

> Specify the _what_ and the _why_ (the intent); leave the _how_ to the executor's judgment; act decisively within intent, escalating only when the situation is genuinely unknown.

Decentralized execution: a competent subordinate, given intent, is _expected_ to act on judgment without seeking approval — waiting for permission is itself a doctrine failure. Supplies the **agency** prior that the design-craft influences ([[hickey]] · [[alexander]] · [[hoare]] · [[fowler]]) do not.
