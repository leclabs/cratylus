# Reader-Prior Projection

The density of a projection is not a property of the idea — it is a function of the **gap between what the idea means and what the reader already holds** ([[latent-priors]]). The same anchor projects differently per reader: for one who already carries the prior, the name alone suffices ([[densest-faithful-point]] at its limit — adding the delineation raises no fidelity); for one who lacks it, the delineation is what closes the gap.

This is the projection-side companion to [[anchor-to-the-readers-priors]] — that one re-anchors _which_ name to use for an audience; this one sets _how much_ to spell out. Sharp form: **delineation size = the reader-gap; → 0 for a reader who already holds the prior.**

The floor is [[anchor-legibility-budget]]: drop to the bare name only where the anchor is a legible pointer the reader can dereference. An idiosyncratic coinage with no shared prior, or a weak reader, keeps the delineation — so the density drop is gated by **reader-strength × anchor-legibility**, never unconditional. Whether a given drop is safe is decided by [[decision-identity]].

Dereferenceability is necessary but **not sufficient**. A disposition that must **override a strong competing base-prior** loses at inference even when its anchor is perfectly legible — _can-dereference_ ≠ _wins-against-a-competing-prior_. Such a disposition is not density-collapsible: it projects with **verbatim salience** (the `render: verbatim` organ path — its operative body emitted whole, density-immune), never as a bare anchor. This is a third gate on the floor, independent of reader strength — and so `render: verbatim` is a legitimate carrier for a contested _disposition_, not only an identity protocol ([[recommendation-style-consensus-quality-pick]] is the proven instance: it must beat the helpful-options reflex).

The dereference channel need not be **inline**. The floor asks only that the reader *can* dereference — and a reader who lacks the prior may still reach it **out-of-band**, through an agent teacher, rather than through prose on the surface. So an **agent-audience artifact** — read by agents that hold the prior, with humans served by a reachable teacher — drops to bare anchors for *both*: comprehension is **relocated to the teacher, not the page**. The guard stays the floor's, now over two channels (in-band prior **or** reachable teacher): drop only where some channel genuinely closes the gap; absent any channel, the delineation stays.
