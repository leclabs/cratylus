# `VISION.md` still carries the enumeration `cratylism` was generalized away from

> **Surfaced, not fixed.** `CANON.md` is explicit: on inter-artifact conflict, revise **MODEL**,
> _surface_ a **VISION** conflict, **never unilaterally edit it**. This is that surfacing.

## Symptom

`a2205eb` generalized the First Principle's reach from an enumeration to every authored surface:

```
∀ authored-surface ⟨σ* ∨ ⊕σ* · prose ≡ identifier ≡ path⟩
```

`VISION.md:127` — the document **above** `cratylism` in the apex order — still reads:

> Canonical names—including anchors, dimensions, skills, agents, files, and directories—should
> therefore be discovered through the same cold-verification discipline.

The generalization landed in the principle and never reached the ground the principle answers to.

## Why this is not merely cosmetic

`CANON.md` names VISION · MODEL · cratylism "the apex triad that must stay **mutually consistent**".
The two statements are not contradictory — `including` is inclusive, so VISION is _weaker_, not
_wrong_ — but they are **two homes for one clause**, and the weaker home is the higher one. That is
the exact shape this plan exists to un-braid, sitting in the apex triad itself.

**The cost is measured, not hypothetical.** On 2026-08-04 the enumeration was quoted back as the
live scope of the axiom and used to rule a naming question **out of scope**; the correct reading
inverted the conclusion. The immediate cause was a stale deployed projection
([`deployed-drifts-from-rendered-unwatched.md`](./deployed-drifts-from-rendered-unwatched.md)), but
the enumeration was reconstructible from VISION alone — so repairing the deployment does **not**
close this. A reader who consults only the apex documents can still derive the retired reach.

This is `entailment-not-vocabulary` at ground level: the premise was generalized, and a downstream
statement that _entails the narrower reading_ survived, invisible to any search for the old wording.

## Shape of the fix — the operator's call, not mine

Three admissible outcomes, in the order I would rank them:

1. **Drop the list from VISION.** The paragraph already says "Canonical names … should be discovered
   through the same cold-verification discipline". The enumeration adds no constraint and is the
   only part that can go stale.
2. **Replace it with the generalized clause**, so the apex document states the reach and `cratylism`
   signifies it — one home, two registers.
3. **Keep it and mark it as illustrative**, which costs a sentence and leaves the invitation to
   misread it as a bound.

Nothing about the choice is mine to make. What is mine is the observation that (3) leaves a live
re-derivation path for a retired scope.

## Acceptance

- `VISION.md` and `cratylism.ts` state one reach, in one place, at their respective registers.
- A cold reader given only the apex triad cannot derive a bounded enumeration of what σ\* governs.
- Ideally a gate: the apex triad's claims about **reach** agree. Nothing checks any cross-document
  consistency in the triad today — which is why this survived a `LOCKED` file being edited.
