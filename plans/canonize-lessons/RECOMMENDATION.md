# The correct way — lessons canonize, they do not accrete in memory (nico)

## Diagnosis: why re-teaching was needed

The session's lessons had **no projected, gated home**. At best they would land — via `dream` — in nico's
private `PROCEDURAL` memory, which is (a) **agent-scoped** (does not bind the fleet), (b) **un-gated** (drifts at
seams — I carried `cold-decode-oracle` + `llm-native` in my SOUL and _still_ regressed on prompts, signs, and
warm≡cold), (c) **downstream of the canon**, not part of it. A principle in memory is _understood_, not
_enforced_. Understanding is not encoding. The dream ritual (censused) routes generalized wisdom
`⇒ PROCEDURAL` (private) — it routes lessons AWAY from the canon, never INTO it. That is the defect.

## The rule: route a lesson by the KIND of truth it is

- **Agent-INTRINSIC** (this individual's identity/quirk/relapse) → per-agent `SEMANTIC`/`PROCEDURAL` (unchanged).
- **PROJECT/SYSTEM truth** (binds ≥1 agent-type or the fleet; a mechanism/governance fact) → **PROMOTED into the
  projected canon**, at the STRONGEST available enforcement seam:

  1. **a GATE** — an accept-gate leg or a test. Mechanically enforced; cannot drift. _(COLD-BLIND, SYMBOLS,
     `census-grounds-spec`.)_ **Prefer this.**
  2. **a PROJECTED CELL** — a skill `formalBlock` or organ value; every agent inherits it via projection.
     _(the `llm-native` fragment's dispatch clause, the `praxis` cell.)_
  3. **a GOVERNING DOC** — `CANON`/`MODEL`/`ENGINE`, for a constitutional fact not reducible to a cell/gate.
     _(the `llm-native ≻ VISION ≻ MODEL` confidence order.)_

  After promotion, `projection-carries(i)` becomes true ⇒ the lesson **DROPS from private memory** (DRY: the canon
  is the single home).

## The through-line (the enforcement preference)

**Prefer a GATE over prose — a gate binds, prose drifts.** Every promotion reaches for the strongest seam it can;
prose in a doc is the last resort, for facts no gate/cell can hold. This is why the three fixes we made by hand
this session are durable (`census-grounds-spec`→praxis gate; σ\*-prompt→llm-native cell) and why the ones still in
memory are not.

## Institutionalize it — the promotion route belongs in `dream`

`dream` is the consolidation seam where a lesson is triaged for its home. Add the split there so promotion is the
DEFAULT, not an ad-hoc heroic:

```
generalized-wisdom(i) ∧ ¬projection-carries(i) ∧ canon-truth(i) ⇒ i ↦ CANON-PROMOTION
generalized-wisdom(i) ∧ ¬projection-carries(i) ∧ ¬canon-truth(i) ⇒ i ↦ PROCEDURAL
CANON-PROMOTION(i) ≜ author i into its strongest seam (gate ≻ cell ≻ governing-doc) ; ⟨¬canon-remit ⇒ emit a
                      canon-candidate task to the curator⟩ ; then projection-carries(i) ⇒ drop from PROCEDURAL
canon-truth(i) ⇔ i binds ≥1 agent-type ∨ the fleet ∨ is a mechanism/governance fact ⟨¬ this-agent-only⟩
```

Not every agent may edit the canon: a non-curator's dream EMITS a canon-candidate (a task for nico), rather than
editing canon directly. nico's dream promotes directly.

## Consequence

With this route, "bringing an agent up to speed" is structurally unnecessary: a project-truth learned by ANY agent
flows to the projected+gated canon, where EVERY agent inherits it (and a gate enforces it) — instead of dying in
one agent's private memory. The three specific lessons still un-encoded (below) are the backlog this route would
have caught; they are the plan's shards.
