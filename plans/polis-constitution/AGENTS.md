# polis-constitution (Phase A)

**Goal.** Make polis's latent sociology *explicit, authored corpus* — the constitution every
mind-society is founded on. Today the sociology is implicit and partial (SOUL/SELF/MEMORY/EPISODIC,
scope-grants, the role roster, commons-vs-instance, marks). This plan names it.

**Scope.** `packages/mind/ideas/` cells only — pure signify/conceptualize, Nico's craft, no code.
Routes through `[[exemplify]]`; mints exemplars where `[[semantic-partition]]` finds homeless primitives.

**Out of scope.** The projection machinery (→ polis-machinery) and the instantiation operations
(→ polis-instantiation). This plan authors *what is true of a mind-society*, not how it is built.

**Exit criteria.**
- The founder charter, role roster, Operator relation, consent-adoption principle, and
  foundational-structure cells exist as corpus, each at its `[[densest-faithful-point]]`.
- `verify.py` PASS (schema + references + fences + round-trip).
- `accept(F)`: the constitution reconstructs equivalent-or-better from the routed cells + deltas.
