**Goal.** Harden the corpus and its projection after founding — sweep the idea-corpus to the current
conventions, crystallize the projection insights this session proved, and propagate + protect the result.

**Scope.** `packages/mind/ideas/**` (cells) · `packages/mind/references/**` (notation) ·
`packages/mind/toolkit/**` (projector, where a sweep needs gate support) · the fleet (deploy) · repo
markdown tooling (formatter). Not a founding phase — the ongoing refinement backlog.

**Lead.** Nico (corpus + projection semantics); Mav for machinery/infra tasks (composer gate support,
Prettier config, fleet deploy).

**Exit criteria.**

- Every formal cell uses the Bindings-region cite-once convention (the CITE-TWICE NOTE promotable to FAIL).
- The verbatim-salience rule is canon in the corpus, not folklore in an agent's memory.
- The salience fix is deployed fleet-wide.
