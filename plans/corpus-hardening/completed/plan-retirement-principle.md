## Intent

## The policy (decided 2026-06-17)

A plan is a **transient execution scaffold** (`doc-mirrors-runtime-truth` applied to work). Retire (delete)
a completed plan once **(a)** its result is in the source of truth AND **(b)** its durable *rationale* has a
permanent home (code, `AGENTS.md` runbooks, the corpus itself). git history is the recovery net — the deleted
plan is always recoverable, so nothing is lost. Keeping completed plans around is palimpsest — a reader infers
live work where there is none.
