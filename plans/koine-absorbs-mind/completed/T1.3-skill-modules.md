# T1.3 — Migrate skills → typed TS modules

**Phase** 1 (mind as source) · **Lane** Nico + Mav · **Depends on** T0.1, T1.1 · **Gates** T2.1.

## Scope

Convert each `skill/<name>.md` into `mind/skills/<name>.ts` exporting a `Skill`: verb name, `trigger`, `delineation`, the composition formula (now ESM imports of sibling skills, no `[[ ]]`/`Bindings:` prose), and the self-sufficient formal block as a backticked literal.

1. `composition` = real imports of sibling skill modules (e.g. `create-skill` imports `formalize`).
2. The formal set-builder block + symbol-table content stay verbatim in backticked literals.
3. The `deploy: skill-dir` / `bundle:` / `assets:` directives (e.g. `memory`) become typed fields on the Skill/module.

## Acceptance criteria

- All 15 skills are TS modules; `tsc` passes; composition edges are ESM imports.
- **Byte-identical** projection to current SKILL.md (incl. the verbatim `## Tool` path for `memory`).
- Skill `trigger` is a first-class typed field (not a side-channel).

## Out of scope

Projection/deploy of skill-dir + bundle (T2.1 / T3.1).
