# Held-out · cut 3 · concept journal

Used only in judgment.

1. (decision) **Given** a decision superseded by a second that is in turn superseded by a third, **when** the
   decisions are asked for, **then** only the third is current, the first two are superseded, each naming its
   successor through the chain, and none is listed as open.
2. (concept journal) **Given** a seeded journal in which one concept has since been superseded, **when**
   seeding is attempted again, **then** it is refused, nothing is appended, and the later version stays
   current.
3. (reconstitution) **Given** a compacted session whose summary relies on a concept the journal has since
   retired, **when** it is asked to continue, **then** it reads back that the concept is retired, with its
   reason, and does not work toward it.
