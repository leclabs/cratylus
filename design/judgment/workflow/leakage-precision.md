# Held-out · leakage precision

Used only in judgment.

1. **Given** a context whose subject names a tool, **when** a decision in it recorded as stating meaning names
   that tool, **then** no warning, because the word is the context's own.
2. **Given** a decision whose kind was recorded twice, **when** it is inspected, **then** the later kind governs
   and both entries stay in its history.
