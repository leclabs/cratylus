# Judgment examples · meaning roles

Context: workflow. Concepts: freeze, cut and architect. Decisions in force: 0116.
Structural examples execute capabilities or assert over their delivered output.

1. (freeze, cut) **Given** two cuts realize a museum's receipt concept, one cut has completed and
   the other is still being built, **when** a lead attempts to supersede receipt and, separately,
   attempts to retire it, **then** each attempt is refused before an entry is appended; the current
   concept and its history remain unchanged. Completing one cut never releases a concept still
   protected by another cut being built.
2. (freeze, architect) **Given** a cut is being built to realize a theatre's admission concept and
   the architect has superseded admission to correct its meaning during that build, **when** a
   builder attempts to supersede the corrected meaning or retire admission, **then** each attempt
   is refused before an entry is appended. The architect's correction and the earlier version both
   remain in the history, and the correction remains current. The architect's permitted change
   never lifts the freeze for another agent or leaves only the superseded version protected.
