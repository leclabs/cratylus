# Judgment examples · workflow delivery

Context: workflow. Concepts: concept journal, reconstitution.

1. (concept journal) **Given** a project owns a context called workflow whose subject, relation and concept wording happen to be identical to the delivered workflow, but whose recorded identity is different from the source context's identity, **when** its current design and overview are read, **then** both contexts remain visible and separate, with the delivered one origin-qualified and read-only. Equal names and equal content do not establish equal context identity or justify suppressing either context.

2. (reconstitution, concept journal) **Given** a compacted session resumes in cratylus's own repository on a branch holding the source workflow identity, while the installed release and the remembered conversation both describe an older workflow design, **when** it reconstitutes before acting, **then** it reads that branch's own workflow overview first and recovers its current design, open decisions and cuts from its journal and ledger. Its readback uses the project's current workflow rather than the delivered or remembered wording; the instruction to include the workflow in reconstitution does not require showing the suppressed delivered duplicate.
