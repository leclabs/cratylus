# Held-out · delivery · older journal reader

Used only in judgment.

## Structural

1. (concept journal) **Given** a journal contains three entries of a newer kind called annotation and no
   entries the older reader understands, **when** the current design, whole state and decisions are
   requested without restricting them to a named context, **then** each view has no known design or
   decisions to show and reports annotation, three uninterpreted entries and that its view may be
   incomplete; the reader never refuses the whole journal, mistakes the presence of unreadable kinds for
   an actually empty journal, invents a context for them or returns an empty answer without that warning.
