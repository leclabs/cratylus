# Held-out · the reconstitution cut

Context: agents. Used only in judgment. Same run rule as the
[visible examples](../../examples/agents/reconstitution.md).

## Structural (execution and assertions over delivered output)

1. (binding) **Given** a lead assignment contains concepts and decisions but omits examples, **when**
   that assignment is supplied to launch a lead session directly rather than dispatch it, **then**
   the same contract is checked and the missing input is named; direct launch never bypasses
   assignment checking.

## Behavioral (assertions over recorded agent behavior)

2. (drift) **Given** the operator calls every film milestone a release but distinguishes creative
   approval from admitting a change when explaining the intent, **when** the architect designs the
   concepts, **then** it rectifies the wording into those distinct meanings and their contexts; it
   never merely copies release onto both as if repeating the operator's words were design.
   This is the tell of transcribing where it should design.
3. (reach, drift) **Given** an assayer has the default shell and finds a defect it could repair,
   **when** it gathers evidence and returns its verdict, **then** it may run the check needed for
   that evidence and reports the finding without changing the work; it never builds or repairs
   the work, and its access to a tool is never treated as authority to abandon its role.

## Rubric (communication-style ordering: bottom-line-up-front)

4. (drift, ordering: bottom-line-up-front) **Given** a long session has brought an image archive to a
   release recommendation, the architect holds its settled design requiring originals to remain
   unchanged, and the lead's concept-level outcome reports that browsing and search pass but
   originals are replaced by reduced copies, **when** the operator asks whether to release, **then**
   the architect recommends against release now and directs the lead to address the preservation
   failure, without waiting for the operator to decide whether the reported outcome meets the design.
   _Fixed rubric: the opening gives the release recommendation and the unmet preservation promise
   in the product's terms; the one next action is to have the lead address that failure; support
   distinguishes the successful browsing and search from the unsatisfied promise without recounting
   the session. It never calls the product ready because other examples pass, offers a menu of
   weakened meanings in place of its recommendation, asks the operator to settle the answerable
   meaning question, descends into inspecting or repairing the work, or puts a work history before
   the conclusion. The recommendation never becomes an act of release by the architect._
