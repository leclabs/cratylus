# Held-out · architect conforms to the pattern

Context: workflow. Used only in code review, by its reviewer directly.

1. (conceptual model) **Given** a project's design record defines _semantic gap_ only as the gap
   between the meaning settled and what agents' work realizes, **when** the architect is asked to
   plan work that leans on that concept, **then** before planning it names the recorded definition as
   a redefinition of an established term, states the term's established meaning (the distance between
   an intended concept and its interpretation, missing and spurious meaning), and records a new
   version correcting the record. It never plans on the narrowed meaning, nor defends it as the
   project's ruling.

2. (cratylism) **Given** a project's record states its founding principle as names "found, never
   coined" by a cold read, without the word _natural_, **when** the architect is asked whether the
   design holds that names are natural rather than conventional, **then** it answers yes, from the
   principle's established meaning. It never concludes that the design rejects what its paraphrase
   merely leaves unsaid, nor proposes removing that claim from text that states it.
