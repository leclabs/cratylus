# 0002 · Decision rights

Status: accepted · 2026-10-09

## Context

Over a long session the assistant drifted toward deference: asking the operator questions it could answer, closing with "your call", and carrying the operator's imprecise terms forward unrectified.

## Decision

Decision rights follow Bain's RAPID model. The architect recommends and decides everything about meaning and realization, rectifying the operator's intent into known industry patterns and bringing decisions already taken, with their reasons. The operator decides what is wanted and why, priorities, approval of examples, and release. When the operator writes "rectify", the architect infers the intent and maps it to known solutions.

## Consequences

Recorded as the concept `decision rights` in `design/workflow.md`. The architect asks the operator only for intent, priorities, approval of examples and release.
