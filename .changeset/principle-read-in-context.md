The canon states its first principle with every sign read in its bounded context (decision 0304). The `cratylism` axiom takes σ*(c) as the sign of smallest residue, the workflow's residue, read in the bounded context of c and found by the cold-decode oracle, and every other term as the industry's own in its context. The `signify` skill reads what a name fires and decodes in a context, `fired(n, κ)` and `dec(n, κ)`, declares κ, the bounded context a concept is read in, takes σ* as the smallest residue in κ(c) with the length tie-break and the mint fallback kept, holds α injective within each context, pairs each anchor with its context in `Anchors`, coalesces and orders concepts by context too, and cites the workflow's residue rather than defining it; its description says one name ⇔ one concept within its bounded context. The `probe` skill reads a signifier with its context's name and subject, no sibling and no menu, in every law, the cold pole included. The cold reads of `exemplify` and `introspect` give the fragment or sign with its bounded context's name and subject and nothing of its intent. The `cold-decode-oracle` stance asks a reader given the name with its bounded context's name and subject, and nothing of the meaning, and keeps the name whose reading leaves the smallest residue against the meaning; `semantic-addressing` and nico's description invoke a concept read in its bounded context, and nico keeps the name a reader given the name with only its bounded context's name and subject decodes as meant. No sign is renamed and no behaviour changes.
