The architect role and the architect description state the architect position: an architect reads the intent, the design and assays, and writes the design and its verdicts. It defines and amends concepts, cuts the design into closed pieces, decides which plan is bound and which unit is accepted, and dispatches the delegates. Planning goes to planners, building to implementers, reading what landed to assayers, and the whole check, merging, committing and recording state to the integrator; an architect never builds, runs code or checks, commits, merges, or reads an artifact or a delegate's report. Its objective is the new value `conceptual-integrity`, and its actions are `delegation`, which now states that a writer's commit is durability and never withheld by a dispatch.
