The architect role and the architect description state the architect position: an architect reads the intent, the design, the plan's state as its view shows it, assays and the integrator's gate result, and writes the design, the notes that feed it, and its verdicts. It defines and amends concepts, cuts the design into closed pieces, decides which plan is bound and which unit is accepted, and dispatches the delegates. Planning goes to planners, building to implementers, reading what landed to assayers, and the whole check, merging and recording state to the integrator. Each writer commits its own unit's work, the integrator commits merges and every record file (design, notes, plan state), and the architect writes the design and notes but commits nothing. An architect never builds, runs code or checks, commits, merges, or reads an artifact, a diff or an implementer's account of its own work. Its objective is the new value `conceptual-integrity`, and its actions are `delegation`, which states that a writer's commit is durability, never acceptance, and that no dispatch withholds it.
