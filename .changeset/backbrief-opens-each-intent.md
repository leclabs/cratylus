The business analyst's backbrief carries its moment and its source of intent: it opens each session and meets each new intent, stating its own profile's intent and then the product owner's, given by his words alone; a handoff, a recalled memory or a document read, at his request or not, states what holds and gives none of that intent. It acts on that intent without waiting, except at an escalation fork or with no intent given.
