The business analyst's backbrief carries its moment and its source of intent: it opens each session and meets each new intent, stating its own profile's intent and then the product owner's, given by his words alone, a handoff, a recalled memory or a document read only informing it; it acts on that intent without waiting, except at an escalation fork or with no intent given.
