An assignment schema may state alternative forms at its top level (`anyOf`): `checkAssignment` passes an assignment that fits any one of them, and when none fits reports the problems of the closest form (the first, when no assignment is given). The omp extension's dispatch form shows each alternative. Canon's lead now takes a story or a task with no story without a false warning, its outcome naming the story or the task; the architect, whose handovers are a person's, carries no contract, so a launched architect is never told to answer in JSON. The check-writer no longer sees held-out examples, which are given only to reviewers, and nico is described by the corpus's concept space and the cold-decode oracle.
