The practice that installs the skills of cratylic context design is now `cratylic-context-design`, described as that practice, in place of `corpus-authoring` (decision 0332). A practice declares the names it was offered under before (`Practice.formerNames`, reported as `OfferedPractice.formerNames`); an install over an earlier one that recorded a former name keeps the practice under its new name, records the new name only, and reports `carried over <old> as <new>`.

BREAKING CHANGE: `--practices corpus-authoring` is refused as an unknown practice; name `cratylic-context-design`.
