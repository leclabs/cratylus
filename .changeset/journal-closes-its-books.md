The concept journal closes its books as event sourcing does. `cratylus concepts close`, and the concept journal tool built from the capability's explanation, opens a new period with one opening entry per current version of each standing item, written at one moment past every entry the journal holds and in the order the readings list them: the project's purpose, each context with its `identity`, each live concept as a `define`, and each standing decision as a `decide`, accepted where it is accepted and with its governing kind where one is recorded. Each opening is an ordinary entry of its item's kind, superseding nothing and carrying `broughtForward`, the full sha of the commit at HEAD that holds the closed period; competing versions each open as an entry of their own, so they still compete. Retired concepts, superseded or deprecated decisions, acceptances, classifications and a cut's efficiency figures stay in the closed period. The close then removes every other entry file, kept by version control at that commit, and prints the commit with how many openings it wrote and how many files it removed. Every reading reads the directory's entries and nothing else, so `state`, `design` and `decisions` answer the same after a close as before; `history`, `design --history` and `decisions --history` carry `booksClosed`, `{ "at", "by", "commit" }`, and mark each opening they list `broughtForward`. A context's identity is the `identity` its earliest entry carrying one carries, else its earliest entry's id, and a journal owns a delivered context whose identity is one of its contexts'. A line of work that branched before a close joins it as recorded: a version superseding versions the journal no longer holds competes with its item's opening, and an acceptance, deprecation or supersession naming a decision entry it no longer holds binds by the decision's name where one decision has it. A concept retired before a close returns by `define`. A close refuses, changing nothing, outside a repository or without a commit at HEAD, while `design/journal` differs from HEAD's tree in anything, an ignored file included, while it reads past entries of a newer runtime, when the journal holds no entry, and when it holds nothing but opening entries; where writing an opening or removing a file fails, it restores the journal from HEAD and removes the openings it wrote. The verb's explanation gains the effect `closes`, which the omp tool approves as a write.
