The concept journal closes its books. `cratylus concepts close`, and the concept journal tool built from the capability's explanation, appends one entry of kind `close` naming the commit at HEAD that holds the closed period, bringing forward verbatim the entries that stand (the current purpose, each context's current descriptions and identity, each concept's current versions unless it stands retired, and each standing decision with its acceptances and governing classification) and the identities of contexts and concepts that keep the readings' order, then removes every other entry file, which git keeps at that commit. Every reading folds the brought-forward entries with the period's own, so `state`, `design` and `decisions` answer the same after a close as before; the history readings carry `booksClosed`, naming the latest close and its commit. A close refuses, changing nothing, outside git or with no commit, while the journal differs from HEAD, while it reads past entries of a newer runtime, with no entry, or with nothing recorded since the latest close. A verb's explained `effect` is now `reads`, `appends` or `closes`; the omp tool asks for write approval for a verb that closes.
