The concept journal warns, and never refuses, where a wording tells more than what holds now. A live concept's wording and a context's current subject and relation are warned where they cite a decision by its number (rule `cites a decision`), recount their history, what changed or what something used to be or no longer is (`recounts history`), or only extend the version they replace, keeping its wording whole and adding to it (`only extends the version it replaces`, the words being what was added). The warnings show in `concepts leakage` and in the answer of `concepts context`, `define` and `supersede`; a context's description is named as the passage `{ "kind": "context", "context", "field": "subject" | "relation" }`.
