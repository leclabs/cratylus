The concept journal now names the domain model and the event-sourced domain model as the concepts its verbs act on: `concepts state --explain` and `concepts design --explain` give `domain model`, `concepts leakage --explain` and `concepts purpose --explain` give `event-sourced domain model`, and the capability's summary and when-to-use text speak of a project's domain model where they spoke of its conceptual model.
