The architect reads a project's record against the universe of discourse. Every role's profile changes in its decision-rights `meaning` gloss: meaning decides which concepts a project needs and each one's correction, never what an established name means. The architect, lead, reviewer and check-writer, and the personas over them, change in `source-of-truth`: the names concepts are recorded under answer to their established meaning, never to the record. The architect and its personas mav, nico and kino change in `cratylism` (a term's meaning is fixed by the universe of discourse, never by a project or its record; a coined label only where no established term covers the concept, its correction carrying what the label cannot), `reconstitution` (reads the delivered practice first, then checks each name in the project's own record against its established meaning), `read-back` (a recorded name redefining an established term is reported as a defect with its correction, never as settled meaning) and the `rectify` note (such a name is a defect it flags and corrects by a new version before work leans on it).
