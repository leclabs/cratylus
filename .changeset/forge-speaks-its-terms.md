forge names what it projects in the design's terms. Renamed exports: the catalog's `PluginFragmentRoot` → `PluginValueRoot` (`fragmentsDir` → `factorsDir`), `FragmentEntry` → `ValueEntry` (`dimension` → `factor`), `PluginFragmentCatalogs` → `PluginValueCatalogs`, `enumeratePluginFragmentCatalogs` → `enumeratePluginValueCatalogs`, `DuplicateNamespaceError.fragmentsDirs` → `factorsDirs`; resolve's `Fragment` → `ValueNode`; `scanCellDirNames` → `scanDeclarationDirNames`; `dimensionField` → `factorField`; `core/exemplify/dimension-fields` → `core/exemplify/factor-fields` (`dimensionFieldsOf` → `factorFieldsOf`) and `core/exemplify/skill-cell` → `core/exemplify/skill-body` (`renderSkillCellBody` → `renderSkillBody`); `discoverFragments` → `discoverValues`; `ProjectablePlugin.fragments` → `factors`; `Realizable.anchor` and `Loss.anchor` → `term`; `HarnessAdapter.hookCommand`'s first parameter is the hook's id. The `install` option `corpus` (of `runInstall` and `projectorCommands`) is now `canon`. `cratylus install` describes itself as installing the bundled canon, and its refusals, prompts and the omp extension speak of the canon, its factors, values, terms and corrections.
