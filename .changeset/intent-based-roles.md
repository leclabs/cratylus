The agents now realize intent-based delegation. The assayer is gone; a `reviewer` reviews one change it did not write, before or after it joins as its story's risk calls for, against the change and, where they apply, its story's acceptance criteria and examples, held-out ones included, and hands over one review, never building or repairing the work; it runs on another model family than the builders where one is available. The architect receives intent and hands over stories and read-backs; the lead owns a story's realization, or a task with no story, orchestrates its tasks to builders and their changes to review as its story's risk calls for (Ship, Show or Ask), joins each change by the project's own integration once every check passes and any review due before it joins approves it, and hands over the outcome; a builder receives one task and hands over one change; the illustrator and check-writer are specialists called in for a story's risk.

Each handover's contract now states what it must contain, so an incomplete one is warned with what is missing: the lead's story (`story`, `slice`, `why`, `acceptanceCriteria`, `outOfScope`, `path`, `review`, `cost`; optional `examples`, `decisions`, `contexts`), the builder's task (`task`, `goal`, `changes`, `decisions`, `done`; optional `story`) and change (`change`, `tests`, `verified`), the reviewer's review request (`change`; optional `story`, `acceptanceCriteria`, `examples`) and review (`verdict` approve or changes, `findings`, `meaningQuestions`), the lead's outcome (`story`, `criteria`, `meaningQuestions`, `followUps`, `cost`), the architect's read-back (`whereThingsStand`, `recommendation`), and the check-writer's and illustrator's handovers keyed by story.

Procedures name industry practices: orchestrator-workers, Ship/Show/Ask, code review and test-first replace build, freeze, judgment and the whole check. The values serving only the retired cut workflow are deleted (the cut, work-package and judgment briefs, the per-concept verdict, done-or-failing-case, outcome-with-efficiency, verdict accuracy, own work package and the `check-ruling` decision right), and the architect's `decisions-with-reasons` deliverable gives way to its read-back; joining each change is part of realization, and release is making merged work available to its users.
