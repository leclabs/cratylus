A project's own copy of a delivered context is read in the delivered context's place (decision 0320). `concepts state` and `concepts design` list under `practice` every delivered context in the delivered order, upstream first; where the project's journal holds one itself, its own copy stands in that place, named by its own name, without `delivered` or `readOnly`, part of the project's design and recorded into as any of its contexts. `contexts` lists the project's other contexts in record order. A project holding cratylic context design but not the workflow now reads its own cratylic context design, then `cratylus:workflow`, then its other contexts; `--context` naming an own copy reads it alone under `practice`.
