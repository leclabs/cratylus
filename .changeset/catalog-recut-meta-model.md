The dimension meta-model is re-cut for the agent catalog (decisions 0044, 0046). `DimensionMeta` is now `{ group, repertoire, arity, level, projection, slots?, required? }`: a free-text `group` replaces the `axis` and its `Genus` type, which are removed; `Repertoire` gains `'fixed'` (a closed list the design fixes); `Arity` gains `'slots'` (one value per named `Slot`) and `'attribute'` (no field on an agent; its values are attributes of other values); `level` is the default `EnforcementLevel` (`'advisory' | 'firm' | 'fixed'`, or `null`) and `projection` says whether the dimension is rendered as `'text'` or compiled to harness settings (`'binding'`); `required` is `true` or absent. `DimensionFieldsOf`/`AgentOf` give every non-attribute dimension a `readonly Entry<D>[]` field, where an `Entry` is `{ value, level, slot?, note? }`, with no `null` (an explicitly empty dimension is `[]`). The `Agent` identity face is `name`, `description`, `mark: Mark | null`, a reserved `key: null`, and optionally `holds`, `dispatches`, `routing: { tier, effort }`, `readOnly`, `skills` and `isolation`; `archetype`, `preamble` and `provenance` are removed.
