The delivered agents assume none of cratylus's own conventions (decisions 0267-0274). A task hands over a change: the builder's deliverable `pull-request` is now `change` (`change`, `tests`, `verified`), and the reviewer's review request takes `change` in place of `pullRequest`. How a change joins the project's shared work is the project's own convention: the `github-flow` procedure is deleted and the builder states no procedure; the lead's `merge` action is replaced by `integrate`, joining a change by the project's own convention once every check passes and any review due before it joins approves it. Review follows risk as Ship, Show or Ask: the lead's new `ship-show-ask` procedure says Ship joins a change with no review, Show joins it and has it reviewed afterwards, and Ask has it reviewed before it joins; the story states which its risk picks, and the lead picks by risk for a task with no story. There is no second reviewer. The reviewer reviews a change before or after it joins, and the story's `path` and `review` describe the plain or scaled-up path and Ship, Show or Ask. The operator's own review is a gate only on a change it has asked to review before it joins, which the sign-off and verdict glosses now say. The architect records a change's design with the change it belongs to. The runtime's README says history is held by review, never by refusal.
