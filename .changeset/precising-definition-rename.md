A term's precising definition is called by its name. `Slot.correction` becomes `Slot.precisingDefinition` and `HookDeclaration.correction` becomes `HookDeclaration.precisingDefinition`; the canon's manifest gives each decision-rights slot its precising definition under the new field. Rendered agents are unchanged.
