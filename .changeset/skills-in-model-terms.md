The skills and their prime principle speak Cratylic context design in the domain model's terms. A Cratylic name is the term whose meaning, read in the concept's domain, has the smallest symmetric difference from the intended concept, and it enters a text with its precising definition, which states what the name lacks and denies what it adds. In signify that is `Δ(n,c)` (with its two sides, `lacks` and `adds`) and `precising-definition(c)`; probe and materialize cite them from signify. The descriptions of exemplify, formalize, materialize, probe and signify say the same. The semantic-gap wording (semantic gap, missing and spurious meaning, correction) and "universe of discourse" are gone: the model's semantic space, which already fixes each name's meaning, says it. Each skill's laws and behaviour are otherwise unchanged.
