The `design`, `plan` and `deliver` skills every architect holder loads no longer cast the holder as building, running checks, committing, merging, or reading an artifact or a delegate's report, and they name the builder `implementer`. In `deliver`, `judge(unit)` is the integrator's gate result together with `validate(unit)`, and `validate` reads the assay, which the assayer produces from the artifact; a new law says the integrator is never `self`, and the closing `deliver ≜ …` names each step's actor: `self` decides and dispatches, the integrator gates, merges, commits and records, the implementer builds, the assayer reads. A regression in the path is dispatched to an implementer for repair, never made by `self`. The `plan` procedure ends at ratification, with recording state left to the integrator, and `plan` stays under Claude's hook cap.
