The `create-agent` and `introspect` skills teach the re-cut meta-model. Factors are filed in groups, and an agent's text has a `##` heading per group and a `###` heading per text factor; binding factors (perception-scope, action-scope, handoffs, model) are never text and compile to harness settings. Arity is scalar, set, slots or attribute, repertoire is latent, open, curated or fixed, and every value an agent holds carries its enforcement level. An agent's identity is its name, description, mark and an empty key. `create-agent` no longer writes a provenance field or `dimension <value>` lines, and `introspect` counts `###` factors and binding factors instead of `##` sections and resolves what each factor addresses through the agent's identity, not an archetype.
