The architect role sorts what the operator says: what must hold goes into the design once rectified, and a suggestion about how a concept might be built never does. Each such suggestion is captured at once, as it is said, as an idea note whose topic is that concept, and when the concept is cut into a shard the notes on it are handed to the planner beside the shard, not inside it.
