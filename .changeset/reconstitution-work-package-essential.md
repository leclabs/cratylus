On omp the cratylus module offers the work package capability as an essential tool (`loadMode: "essential"`), its whole explanation in every turn's tool list, rather than an `xd://` device whose prompt line is its purpose cut at 200 characters, without its `when` or verbs. That line ended before where the cut stands, so an agent asked where its cut stands never met `state`, the verb that works it out afresh from the ledger. The concept journal stays a device. `OMP_ESSENTIAL_CAPABILITIES` names the capabilities offered whole.
