Projection reads the re-cut meta-model. An agent's text is now `# <emoji> <name>` (or `# <name>` with no mark), its description, then one `## <Group>` per factor group in catalog order and one `### <factor>` per text factor, each held entry a line `- [<slot> (<slot gloss>): ]**<anchor>**[ (<note>)] — <gloss>[ (fixed)]`, and `- (none)` for an empty factor; binding factors and attribute factors are never text, and the archetype, preamble and dimension titles are gone. Every dimension field is read as a list of entries: the fold rewrites an entry's value and keeps its level, slot and note, and the bindings of enforcing values are read off entry values. `ProjectedTree.heldRoles` and `ProjectedTree.roleHolders` are removed for `ProjectedTree.agentModels` (each rendered agent's `routing` tier and effort, by agent name), and a practice is closed under dispatch when a handoff is held by a chosen agent or supplied natively by the harness (`HarnessAdapter.nativeAgents`). `cratylus catalog` and `CatalogEntry` report a dimension's `group` in place of its axis. Agent elevation (`renderAgentVector`/`elevateAgent`) takes a `description`, emits `mark: null` and `key: null`, writes each selected fragment as an entry at its dimension's default level (one per slot for a `slots` dimension, which a fragment names with `slot`), writes `[]` for an inherited or silent dimension, and refuses an `attribute` dimension as a field. The `agent.archetype` residue-gated field is removed.
