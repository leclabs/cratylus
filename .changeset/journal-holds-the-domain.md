The delivered practice is named intent-based delegation, no longer workflow: it hands work to agents by intent and leaves the method to their judgment, applying established practices such as domain-driven design and specification by example. It speaks of the domain model, domain-driven design's own term, where it said conceptual model: the record holds what a project's users observe or rely on, never how the project is built or works inside. A bounded context is cut where the language changes, never by topic, and its subject is one part of the problem domain, core, supporting or generic.
