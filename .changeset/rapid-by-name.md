Decision rights are invoked as RAPID by its name. A factor's metadata may now name the framework its values are the terms of (`framework`), and the projected agent text names it once, at the factor's heading: `### decision-rights (RAPID)`. Canon's decision-rights values `D`, `I`, `R`, `P` and `A` are now bare letters, carrying their meaning by RAPID's name; `–` is not a RAPID letter and keeps its gloss, "has no part". The slots' precising definitions are unchanged.
