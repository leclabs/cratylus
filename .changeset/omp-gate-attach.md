Attach on omp: when a delegate whose decision rights hold sign-off and that declares a deliverable ends its turn on a reply that is not that deliverable, it is holding a gate, and the omp extension gives the dispatching session only a notice that it waits on the operator's sign-off and how the operator joins it in omp's Agent Hub (Alt+A, select the agent, Enter), and later its result. This holds on the task result (content and details), on a background result, and on each later turn the operator prompts in the delegate's session: another gate gives another notice, and the deliverable gives the result. Every other result passes unchanged. The `cratylus` and forge READMEs describe attach on omp and the limits omp sets on it.
