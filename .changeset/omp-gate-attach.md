Attach on omp: an agent whose decision rights hold sign-off is declared `blocking`, and when it ends its turn on a reply that is not its declared deliverable (every turn, for one that declares no JSON deliverable) it is holding a gate. The omp extension then gives the dispatching session only a notice that it waits on the operator's sign-off and how the operator joins it in omp's Agent Hub (Alt+A, select the agent, Enter), and later its result. This holds on the task result (content and details) and on each later turn the operator prompts in the delegate's session, whether the extension hands that turn over or omp does: another gate gives another notice, and the deliverable gives the result. Every other result passes unchanged. The `cratylus` and forge READMEs describe attach on omp and the limits omp sets on it.
