The seven skills of cratylic context design (conceptualize, elicit, exemplify, formalize, materialize, probe, signify) say in their descriptions what each is for and when to reach for it, in the pattern's own terms (term, Cratylic name, semantic gap, missing and spurious meaning, correction, cold read), with no notation and nothing of how cratylus builds them (decision 0334). Their bodies keep their procedures with the design's terms: signify's `residue(n,c)` is now `gap(n,c)`, the semantic gap of n for c read in κ(c) (missing meaning ∪ spurious meaning, cited `@ cratylic context design`), and σ* is the term of smallest semantic gap; a concept enters a text as its term with `correction(c)`, its missing meaning stated and its spurious meaning stated denied, the term alone when the correction is empty; the cold-decode oracle is now the cold read, and a sign or signifier meaning a name is a term. Only what the design defines (the semantic gap, the correction, the cold read) is cited to it; exemplify's warm decode, `coldpass` and `K`, and probe's `K`, are the skills' own.

Every concept is held to its Cratylic name, in a user's project as in cratylus's own: the limit to the core agent dimension names and values is gone from every body. signify's `dom(α)` is every concept the text carries, a concept its domain already names takes that established term, found and never coined, its cold read confirming it, and a concept is no longer excluded for being outside that vocabulary; exemplify's `valid(k)` requires the cold pass of every piece, and its `core` is gone; formalize names every entity and operation by its term through signify. materialize writes a primitive concept as its term with its correction, the term alone where the correction is empty, never restating what the term carries, in place of the term with the concept's whole meaning; composites stay by reference.

The prime principle the skills open with states cratylism as the design holds it: names natural to the model, whose semantic space is the universe of discourse; each concept takes its Cratylic name, the term of smallest semantic gap read in its bounded context, which intuition proposes and a cold read confirms, found and never coined unless no established term covers it; its correction beside it, each side of the gap once; a kept name short of it recorded as such. It no longer cites the residue or the oracle, nor scopes itself to the corpus's core agent dimension names and values. The canon README says the same.
