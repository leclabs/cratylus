The seven skills of cratylic context design (conceptualize, elicit, exemplify, formalize, materialize, probe, signify) say in their descriptions what each is for and when to reach for it, in the pattern's own terms (term, Cratylic name, semantic gap, missing and spurious meaning, correction, cold read), with no notation and nothing of how cratylus builds them (decision 0333). Their bodies keep their procedures with the design's terms: signify's `residue(n,c)` is now `gap(n,c)`, the semantic gap of n for c read in κ(c) (missing meaning ∪ spurious meaning, cited `@ cratylic context design`), and σ* is the term of smallest semantic gap; a concept enters a text as its term with `correction(c)`, its missing meaning stated and its spurious meaning stated denied, the term alone when the correction is empty; every citation of the cold-decode oracle is now the cold read, and a sign or signifier meaning a name is a term. The prime principle the skills open with states cratylism as the design holds it: names natural to the model, whose semantic space is the universe of discourse; each concept takes its Cratylic name, the term of smallest semantic gap read in its bounded context, which intuition proposes and a cold read confirms, found and never coined unless no established term covers it; its correction beside it, each side of the gap once; a kept name short of it recorded as such. It no longer cites the residue or the oracle, nor scopes itself to the corpus's core agent dimension names and values. The canon README says the same.
