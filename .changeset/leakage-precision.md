Leakage now warns only where realization replaces meaning (decision 0058). A decision states what it is about: `cratylus concepts decide` takes `--kind meaning` or `--kind realization`, and a decision recorded without it is inspected as stating meaning. A new appending verb, `cratylus concepts classify <id> --kind meaning|realization [--reason <why>]`, records the kind of a decision already recorded as an entry of its own, so the decision's record never changes; `<id>` is the decision's id, or its entry where two decisions share the id, and the latest kind recorded governs. A decision that already has a kind is classified again only with `--reason`; an unknown or ambiguous decision, an unknown kind and an empty reason are refused, and every refusal appends nothing. `decisions` and `state` report each decision's governing `kind` (`null` where none is recorded) and its `kinds`, every kind recorded in order, each with the `entry` that recorded it and its `reason` where one was given. `leakage`, and the warnings on `define`, `supersede` and `decide`, no longer inspect a decision whose kind is `realization`, and a context's own words now include each word its current subject names, so a tool the subject names is no leak as that plain word in that context's concepts, decisions and examples, and still is in any other; a command, a code span or a flag that holds the word is still warned, and a word joined to others in the subject (`omp's`, `--agent`, `vitest-based`) still names its bare word. Changing a context none of whose descriptions is current no longer fails with an uncaught error: a field left out is refused, naming the field to give.
