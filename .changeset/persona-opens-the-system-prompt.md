On omp, the system prompt of an agent cratylus installed, launched with `omp --agent` or dispatched, opens with the agent's definition, and omp's own prompt follows it as omp builds it each turn, without the passages cratylus judged contrary to the persona or bloat (omp's identity line, three lines of its engineering practice, its `Workflow`, `Delivery` and `Critical` sections, the critical element after the project context, and the sentences that push a dispatched child to keep going); every other section is kept, a newer omp's included, and the way a result is handed back stays. A passage the installed omp's prompt no longer holds is named once, in a warning at session start. The prompt of any other agent's session is unchanged.
