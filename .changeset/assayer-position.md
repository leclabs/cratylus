The assayer is the one judge of a unit. It reads the design shard the unit was created to realize and the files at the commit it is given, never the unit's spec or the implementer's account, and returns the assay verdict: achieved, or not achieved with each missing part (the concept, the factor or clause of the shard left uncovered, and an address), naming the unit and its implementer. The three questions a verdict answers (does the artifact spell its concept's anchor, does its behaviour cover the shard exactly with nothing missing and nothing extra, does anything else already realize it) are stated in the assayer's role. Acceptance rests on the verdict, not on the implementer's account, and no opinion on naming, structure or test quality and no recommendation is part of it.
