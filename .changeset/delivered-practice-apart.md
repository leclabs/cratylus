The delivered practice is shown apart from a project's design (decision 0220). `concepts overview`, `state` and `design` list under `contexts` only the project's own bounded contexts, its design and its context map; each context the installation delivers (`cratylus:workflow`) is shown apart, under `practice: { about, contexts }`, labelled as the practice the project's agents work by and placed before `contexts` so a fresh session still reads it first. Its overview's account says it is not part of the project's design and tells its relation in its origin's context map, never the project's; in `state` it carries no empty `decisions` or `efficiency`. A journal holding the delivered context itself, by the entry that first recorded it, reads its own among its `contexts`, as part of its design, and shows no `practice`.

Every view shows a decision by what it says first: `decisions` leads each with its `title`, its `id` and `entry` last as references; `state` lists them by `title`, `kind`, `id` and `entry`; `overview` gives open ones as `{ title, id }` and tells them as `“<title>” (decision <id>)`; and `leakage` names a decision's passage by its `title` as well as its context and id. The omp session-start note says the delivered workflow is shown apart from the project's design and never part of it.
